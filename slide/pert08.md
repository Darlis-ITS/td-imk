## Konteks RPS
UTS: Research Proposal Defense

### Materi/Kegiatan

Proposal mencakup:

- Background.
- Research problem.
- Significance.
- State of the art.
- Research gap.
- Research question.
- Hypothesis jika diperlukan.
- Proposed contribution.
- Methodology.
- Participants/sample.
- Instrument.
- Procedure.
- Measurement.
- Data analysis plan.

### Fokus

Mahasiswa mempertahankan:

> **Mengapa penelitian perlu dilakukan dan mengapa metode tersebut dapat menjawab research question?**
