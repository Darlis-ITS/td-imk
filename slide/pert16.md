### Kegiatan

Presentasi final meliputi:

- Research problem.
- Research gap.
- Research question.
- Methodology.
- Study design.
- Participants/data.
- Results.
- Findings.
- Discussion.
- Scientific contribution.
- Limitations.
- Future work.
- Target publication venue.

### Final Deliverables

1. **Final Research Paper**
2. **Research Presentation & Defense**
3. **Supporting Materials**, jika relevan:
   - questionnaire;
   - interview protocol;
   - experiment protocol;
   - dataset;
   - analysis script;
   - prototype;
   - supplementary materials.
